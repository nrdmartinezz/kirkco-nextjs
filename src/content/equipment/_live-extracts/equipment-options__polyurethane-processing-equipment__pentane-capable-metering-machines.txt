Overview




Pentane Capable Metering Machines for Eco-Friendly Polyurethane Foam Production



Pentane capable metering machines are specialized polyurethane foam metering and mixing systems designed to handle hydrocarbon blowing agents like pentane. Unlike standard PU metering machines, these units are engineered to safely meter, mix, and dispense formulations that include pentane — enabling high-quality closed-cell foam production with reduced environmental impact.
Pentane is commonly used as a blowing agent in rigid and integral skin foams due to its effective foaming characteristics and its suitability for producing high-insulation and structurally robust PU foam.

The importance of Pentane Metering Machines




Why Pentane Metering Machines Matter





Using pentane as a blowing agent offers advantages over older fluorinated blowing agents (like HCFCs) because it has a lower ozone depletion potential and helps manufacturers align with
sustainable production practices.

However, pentane is
flammable and volatile
, so metering equipment must include
appropriate safety systems, ventilation, and process controls
 to manage combustible hydrocarbon vapors safely during processing.

Kirkco’s pentane-capable machines are engineered to support these requirements with integrated monitoring, controls, and customization options that fit complex production environments.















Typical Industrial Applications






Pentane capable metering systems are widely used wherever closed-cell polyurethane foams are required, with formulations that include hydrocarbons, including:

Insulation panels and sandwich structures are used in building construction

Refrigeration and cold-storage foam cores with enhanced insulation properties

Automotive structural foam applications where thermal and mechanical performance matter

Furniture and appliance manufacture for high-R-value foam components

These machines support both continuous and batch polyurethane foam production, accommodating varied product designs and production rates.













How Pentane Capable Systems Work







Material Conditioning &amp; Feed:
 Polyol and pentane blowing agent streams are prepared and conditioned for stable viscosity and pressure.

Controlled Metering:
 Vulcanized metering pumps feed accurate volumes of each component based on recipe requirements.

Mixing:
 Components pass through mix heads that ensure homogeneous distribution of blowing agent and polyol before cure.

Dispensing &amp; Foaming:
 Mixed material exits the mix head into molds or through applicators, where pentane vaporizes to create uniform closed-cell foam structure.

Pentane capable systems often include advanced PLC control and HMI screens for adjusting recipes, monitoring alarms, and logging production data to support quality control initiatives.
