Flow Regulation Solutions for Precision Lubrication &amp; Fluid Delivery





In lubrication and fluid delivery systems, flow regulation ensures that grease, oil, and other fluids move through the system at the right rate — regardless of pressure or pump variation. Stable flow improves performance of downstream metering valves, reduces material waste, and enhances repeatability in automated and manual lubrication processes.

Flow regulation products are essential where consistent, measurable flow directly impacts dispense accuracy, system efficiency, and service life of lubrication equipment.

Flow Regulation Product Families







Flow Regulating Valves





Flow regulating valves control the rate of material flow in lubrication lines independent of changes in pressure. Rather than controlling pressure itself, these valves ensure that output remains stable, which is critical for repeatable dispensing and equitable distribution across multiple lubrication points.







Key Features:






Adjustable flow setpoints for specific applications

Smooth throttling action with minimal pulsation

Durable components suited to grease and oil viscosities

Easy calibration and serviceability










Benefits:






Stable material flow to metering or dispensing valves

Improves consistency across lubrication circuits

Reduces variation from pump performance






Ideal For:






Multi‑zone lubrication systems

Feed lines to precision metering valves

Systems with varying backpressure conditions












Flow Meters





Flow meters provide real‑time measurement of lubricant volume and flow rate passing through a line. In lubrication systems, they are key for verifying delivery, controlling usage, and providing feedback for automated control systems.







Key Features:






Positive displacement gear or paddle wheel sensing for accurate flow measurement

Contactless electronic sensors for pulse output

LED status indicators on selected models

Integrated pulse output for data logging or metering feedback

Compatible with a wide range of greases, oils, and fluids










Benefits:






Confirms actual lubricant flow in real time

Helps prevent over‑ or under‑lubrication

Supports system diagnostics and maintenance tracking






Ideal For:






Automated lubrication systems

Centralized feed lines

Quality monitoring in production environments












Volume Counters





Volume counters track and display the total volume of lubricant that has passed through a flow network. They are especially useful for verifying consumption, validating dispense cycles, and supporting maintenance schedules or reporting requirements.







Key Features:






Totalizer display for cumulative volume tracking

Compatible with pulsed outputs from flow sensors

Clear, easy‑read digital or mechanical counters

Mountable close to lubrication points or metering systems










Benefits:






Tracks lubricant usage over time

Supports preventive maintenance planning

Useful for production quality records or compliance






Ideal For:






Factory lubrication monitoring

Maintenance dashboards

Systems requiring usage verification
